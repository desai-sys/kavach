Privacy policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Kavach Scam Shield ("Kavach") helps people check whether messages, links, files and calls are scams, and alerts family members they choose when they may be in danger. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Your WhatsApp number and profile name, so we can reply to you.
  • Your preferences: language, and optionally your district and bank name, so warnings and advice fit you.
  • Family contacts you add: their name, WhatsApp number and email, so we can alert them.
  • What you send us to check: messages, links, screenshots, PDFs, voice notes and forwarded emails.
  • Check results: the verdict, a short summary, and any alerts sent.
  • Scammer identifiers found in scams (their phone numbers, UPI IDs, account numbers and web domains), to warn others. We never add your own number to this list.

Why we use it

  • To check what you send and reply with a verdict and advice.
  • To alert your chosen family members, and the bank fraud desk if you tell us money was lost, so they can act quickly.
  • To warn other Kavach users in your district when the same scam is spreading.
  • To send your family a weekly summary of checks by email.

We use your data only for these purposes. We do not sell it or use it for advertising.

Consent and your choices

  • Kavach asks for your consent on WhatsApp before checking anything.
  • Send STOP to Kavach at any time to stop the service.
  • You can ask us to show, correct or delete your data by writing to the contact below. We act on requests within 30 days.

Who processes your data

  • Amazon Web Services hosts Kavach in the Asia Pacific (Mumbai) region. AI analysis uses Amazon Bedrock, which may process requests in other AWS regions. Optional email checking runs in the Asia Pacific (Singapore) region.
  • WhatsApp (Meta) delivers messages between you and Kavach.
  • Your family contacts receive alerts about you, and the bank fraud desk receives a report only when you tell us money was lost.

Security

Data is encrypted in storage and in transit. Links are opened in an isolated cloud browser that never fills in forms. Kavach will never ask for your OTP, PIN, password or card details.

Contact and grievances

For questions, data requests or complaints about how your personal data is handled, write to cantrill.desai@gmail.com. This policy is intended to follow India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

If you have lost money to a scam, call 1930 or report at cybercrime.gov.in.
