How it works

1

Forward it

Send Kavach the message, link, screenshot, PDF, or a voice note describing a call.

2

Kavach investigates

An AI agent opens links in a safe sandbox, reads images, checks reported numbers and matches known scam patterns.

3

Clear answer

A verdict in your language, the reasons, and exactly what to do next.

When someone is in danger

Family alert

If a parent is on a fake "police" or "CBI" call, Kavach alerts the family members they chose, and alerts again if nobody responds.

Golden hour

If money was already sent, Kavach prepares the complaint for the 1930 helpline, emails the bank fraud desk and checks back in 15 minutes.

Your safety and privacy

  • Kavach never asks for OTPs, PINs, passwords or card numbers.
  • Kavach asks for your consent before checking anything. Send STOP at any time to turn it off.
  • Files you send are deleted after 7 days. Read the privacy policy.
Lost money to a scam? Call 1930 right away, or report at cybercrime.gov.in. Reporting within minutes gives the best chance of the money being held.
